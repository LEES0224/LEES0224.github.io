I'm a first-year PhD student (NPGS Scholar) at NTU CCDS, advised by Prof. Atsushi Nitanda. My main research focuses on sampling methods, including diffusion-based samplers, bridge and flow-based approaches, and their combination with Monte Carlo methods. I'm also interested in the theoretical foundations and methodology of diffusion and flow-based models, including reward fine-tuning, with tools from optimal transport.

Before my PhD, I completed my B.Eng. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering with a minor in Mathematics at NTU, graduating with Highest Distinction. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Prof. Ruqi Zhang (Purdue) and Prof. Bihan Wen (NTU), and as an AI research intern at NanoforgeAI.

Research

Sampling Methods

Sampling from unnormalized distributions on continuous and discrete domains, with diffusion, bridge and flow-based samplers and their combination with Monte Carlo methods such as annealing and Sequential Monte Carlo.

Generative Models

Diffusion and flow-based models on continuous and discrete state spaces, from both theoretical and methodological perspectives: optimal transport, efficient training and inference, discrete and masked diffusion, and reward fine-tuning toward desired properties.

AI4Science

Generative models and sampling methods across the natural sciences, from materials to biology: crystal and molecular generation, protein design, and sampling from Boltzmann distributions of physical systems.
